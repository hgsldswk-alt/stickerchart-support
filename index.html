How to use

  • On first launch, tap a starter (or Write my own). Your chart opens right away.
  • Tap Add a sticker each time you do it. Tapped by mistake? Tap Undo on the same screen.
  • Tap the chart title to change the name, number of stickers, reward, halfway reward, one-a-day rule, reminder or sticker pack.
  • When the chart is full, claim your reward. Full charts move to My wall.
  • Share card: share your chart as an image, or send it to a friend as a code. They paste it in New › Got a code from a friend.
  • Widget: add the Sticker Chart widget to your Home Screen and tap Add a sticker without opening the app.

Purchase

Free: 2 charts at a time. Everything is open for the first 30 days. One non-consumable purchase unlocks unlimited charts. No subscription. Restore it in Settings › Unlock everything › Restore purchase.

Privacy

Sticker Chart does not collect any data. Your charts stay on your iPhone (shared only with the app's own widget). No account, no analytics, no ads, no server.

简体中文

怎么用

  • 第一次打开，点一个模板（或「自己写一张」），表就建好了。
  • 每做到一次，点贴一枚。点错了就点同屏的撤销。
  • 点表名可以改名字、格数、奖励、中途小奖励、一天一次、每日提醒和贴纸包。
  • 集满后兑现奖励，表会挂进成就墙。
  • 分享卡：把表存成图片分享，或用口令发给朋友；对方在新表 › 朋友给的口令里粘贴。
  • 小组件：把贴纸奖励表小组件放到主屏幕，不用打开 App 也能点贴一枚。

购买

免费同时可用 2 张表，前 30 天全部开放。一次买断（非消耗型）后不限张数，无订阅。可在 设置 › 解锁完整版 › 恢复购买 找回。

隐私

贴纸奖励表不收集任何数据。表只存在你的 iPhone 上（只和本 App 的小组件共享），不用注册，没有统计、广告和服务器。
